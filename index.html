How to play

  1. Choose an emoji and a background color for each player. Take turns playing on the same device.
  2. On your turn, select any empty square to place your emoji.
  3. Get five or more of your pieces in an unbroken horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line to win. A full board with no winning line is a draw.
  4. Use Undo to take back the last move, or New round to clear the board and keep the score.
